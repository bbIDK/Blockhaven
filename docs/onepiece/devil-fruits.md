# Research: Devil Fruits, Haki and Rokushiki

From the One Piece Wiki (onepiece.fandom.com: Devil_Fruit, Seastone, each fruit's page and its
Techniques page, Kenbunshoku/Busoshoku/Haoshoku_Haki, Rokushiki) and the fruit lists of One Piece:
Pirate Warriors 4 and Blox Fruits. Damage numbers are game design, not canon (iron sword = 6).

## Rules every fruit follows (canon)
- Three types: Paramecia (body/substance/force), Zoan (animal forms: human, hybrid, beast; Ancient and
  Mythical are rarer and stronger), Logia (become an element; intangible to normal hits).
- Users can't swim: about half-submerged in any deep water they lose strength (rain is fine).
- Seastone ("the sea in solid form") cancels powers and weakens the user on touch.
- One fruit per lifetime; a second kills the eater. Every fruit tastes awful; the first bite gives the
  power. Fruits have swirl-patterned skins and never rot.
- When a user dies, the power is reborn in a fruit somewhere else in the world.
- Armament Haki hits a Logia's real body; Observation Haki senses and dodges; Conqueror's Haki knocks
  out the weak-willed (born with it, not trained).

## The fruits (look of the fruit, signature techniques)
- Gomu Gomu (Model: Nika), violet round swirled melon: Pistol (long punch), Gatling (barrage), Bazooka
  (double push), Rocket (grab and launch), Fusen (balloon bounces shots), Gear 2 (steam, speed),
  Gear 5 (white, rubberizes the world). Rubber body: no fall damage, blunt hits and lightning bounce
  off; blades still cut.
- Mera Mera, orange flame-shaped pieces: Hiken (fire fist column), Higan (fire bullets), Enjomo (ring
  of fire), Hotarubi, Jujika, Entei. Body of fire: fire and lava don't hurt. Magma beats it.
- Hie Hie (look not on record): Ice Age (freezes the sea), Ice Time (freeze a target), Partisan (ice
  spears), Ice Saber, Pheasant Beak.
- Goro Goro, gold pear with spark spikes: El Thor (lightning pillar from the sky), Vari (discharge),
  Sango, lightning-speed travel. Rubber beats it.
- Suna Suna, tan barrel-cactus: Desert Spada (ground-splitting blade), Sables (sandstorm), Ground
  Secco (dries ground and life). Water makes the sand solid.
- Yami Yami, purple teardrop pieces: Black Hole (swallows the area), Kurouzu (pulls a target),
  Liberation (spits it out); touching cancels others' powers; can't dodge, takes more damage.
- Gura Gura, blue pear, cracked lower half: cracks the air (shockwave punches), quakes, tsunamis.
- Ope Ope, red heart-shaped: ROOM (blue sphere), Shambles (swap places), Takt (lift), Amputate.
- Bara Bara, orange-red berry pieces: immune to cutting; Bara Bara Hou (flying hand).
- Pika Pika (look not on record): lasers, Yata no Kagami (light-speed travel), Amaterasu (flash),
  Yasakani no Magatama (light bullets), Ama no Murakumo (light sword).
- Magu Magu (look not on record): Dai Funka (magma fist), Ryusei Kazan (magma meteor rain),
  Inugami Guren; above fire in the hierarchy.
- Moku Moku, grey cloud pieces: White Snake (smoke grab), White Out (traps all round), smoke flight.
- Hana Hana, pink flower: sprouts arms anywhere; Clutch, Gigantesco Mano.
- Bomu Bomu, dark purple bomb with fuse stem: any body part explodes; immune to explosions.
- Ito Ito, lilac pear: strings; Parasite (puppet control), Goshikito, Overheat, Birdcage.
- Tori Tori Model: Phoenix, green pineapple with flame-coloured leaves: flight, blue healing flames,
  Hoo-in kick.
- Uo Uo Model: Seiryu, blue scaled with horn stems: azure dragon, Bolo Breath fire, flame clouds.
- Hito Hito (Chopper), red-capped mushroom: forms (Walk, Brain, Heavy), Rumble Ball points.
- Mochi Mochi, white puffed mochi: sticky mochi traps, Logia-like dodging, Mochi Tsuki.
- Gasu Gasu (look not on record): poison gas, airless zone, explodes when lit.
The "core set" games share: Gomu, Mera, Hie, Goro, Suna, Yami, Gura, Pika, Magu, Moku, Ope, Ito,
Phoenix, Mochi.

## Haki (looks)
- Observation: red pupils in the anime; senses presence through walls; dodges what it foresees.
- Armament: hardening coats fists and blades black (glossy), raises attack and defence, hits Logias;
  Emission strikes without contact.
- Conqueror's: wave of pressure, black-and-red lightning; the weak faint, animals flee.

## Rokushiki
Soru (vanishing dash), Geppo (air jumps), Tekkai (iron body, can't move), Shigan (finger stab),
Rankyaku (air-blade kick), Kami-e (paper-body dodge).
