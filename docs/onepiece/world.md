# Research: the One Piece world, for the planet generator

From the One Piece Wiki (onepiece.fandom.com: Grand_Line, Calm_Belt, Red_Line, Reverse_Mountain,
Log_Pose, Island_Climatic_Types, Sea_Clouds, Knock_Up_Stream, and each island's page). Build notes
are design choices, not wiki claims.

## Geography and how the planet uses it
- The Red Line is the one continent, a ring round the world; the Grand Line is a sea route round the
  world at right angles to it. They split the rest of the sea into North, East, West and South Blue.
  **Planet (16,384 blocks round, a torus):** the Red Line is a wall of red rock running all the way
  round north-south at x 8,192; the Grand Line is a 3,000-block band round the world east-west at z
  8,192. On a torus two rings cross once: there stands Reverse Mountain, its canal the way through
  the Red Line. Sailing north-south you go all the way round anywhere; east-west, along the Grand Line.
  The quarters round them are the four Blues.
- Calm Belts: strips either side of the Grand Line with no wind or waves, home of the biggest Sea
  Kings (no islands there).
- Paradise is the Grand Line's first half from Reverse Mountain, the New World the second (wilder).
- Grand Line islands are Summer, Spring, Autumn or Winter islands, each keeping its season; the open
  sea between them is chaotic (storms, waterspouts, fog). Islands' magnetism makes compasses useless;
  a Log Pose records an island and points on to the next (New World poses have three needles).
- Sky islands: Sea Cloud (sailable, springy) about 7,000 m up, Island Cloud (solid, cut into houses),
  reached by the Knock Up Stream, a water column shooting into the sky.

## Island archetypes (from the islands researched)
1. East Blue village isle (Foosha, Syrup): grass, oak and plaster cottages, windmill, tavern, pier.
2. Cliff-plateau estate (Syrup Village): cliffs all round but two ramps; mansion on top.
3. Marine fortress island (Shells Town, Loguetown, Marineford): walled harbour, cannon towers, HQ.
4. Desert kingdom (Arabasta): dunes, oasis, river, round walled capital on a mesa, domes, pyramid.
5. Winter drum peaks (Drum): cylinder-shaped snowy mesas, castle on the tallest.
6. Prehistoric volcano jungle (Little Garden): giant trees, a volcano, huge bones.
7. Canal tier city (Water 7): stepped cone, water running down canals, docks, a fountain on top.
8. Mangrove grove cluster (Sabaody): giant mangroves in shallows, bridges, numbered groves, bubbles.
9. Sky island (Skypiea): cloud layer up high, cloud houses, golden ruins, a giant beanstalk.
10. Dual-climate island (Punk Hazard): half burning, half frozen.
11. Walled waterfall nation (Wano): ring wall with waterfalls, volcano, Japanese town and castle.
12. Themed kingdoms: candy (Whole Cake), Gaudí colours and colosseum (Dressrosa), future city
    (Egghead), foggy dead forest (Thriller Bark).
Special places: Reverse Mountain (canal over the Red Line, lighthouse at Twin Cape), Baratie (fish-
head restaurant ship), Enies Lobby (island ringed by waterfalls into a hole), Fish-Man Island.

## Marines, ships, creatures
- Marine bases show the "Marines" kanji and the gull emblem; Marine ships have three masts, cannons
  along both sides and the gull on the front sails. Going Merry: caravel, sheep figurehead. Thousand
  Sunny: brigantine, lion figurehead, lawn deck.
- Sea Kings: huge, every shape, most in the Calm Belts. Momoo (giant sea cow), Yagara Bulls (horse-
  fish taxis of Water 7), Kung-Fu Dugongs, Bananawani, Lapahns (Drum), dinosaurs (Little Garden),
  Den Den Mushi (snail telephones).
