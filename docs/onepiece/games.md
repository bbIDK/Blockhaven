# Research: how One Piece games turn the story into gameplay

From the Blox Fruits, Grand Piece Online, King Legacy and A One Piece Game wikis, Mine Mine no Mi's
own documentation (pixelatedw.xyz), Mine Piece and One Piece World Like (Modrinth), and reviews of
Pirate Warriors 4, Odyssey and Unlimited World Red; swords from the One Piece Wiki.

## What the mod takes from them
- **Finding fruits** (Blox Fruits, GPO, Mine Mine no Mi): fruits turn up under trees now and then and
  rot away if nobody takes them, and come in rare chests; rarity tiers (common to mythical).
- **Moves on fixed keys** (Blox Fruits: Z/X/C/V plus F to move), each with a cooldown and an energy
  cost, more unlocking as you master the fruit by using it. (Mine Mine no Mi's ability bars were
  found awkward; fixed keys read better.)
- **Haki trained by doing** (Blox Fruits, GPO, Mine Mine no Mi): Armament grows by hitting with it
  on, Observation by being hit (dodge charges, outlines through walls), Conqueror's only for the
  rare few born with it. Armament and seastone hit Logias.
- **Counters** (Mine Mine no Mi): rubber shrugs off blunt and lightning, Bara off slashes, water
  beats sand; seastone ore on the deep sea floor, made into weapons and cuffs that stop powers.
- **Pirates and Marines, bounty** (Blox Fruits, GPO, Mine Mine no Mi): bounty from beating Marines
  and bosses, seen on wanted posters; Marines' ranks.
- **Sea events** (Blox Fruits, GPO): Sea Kings in rough waters and the Calm Belts, more of them the
  higher your bounty; ships with cannons, mended with planks.
- **Seas in tiers** (Mine Piece, One Piece World Like): East Blue start, Reverse Mountain into the
  Grand Line, the New World beyond; Log Pose as the compass.
- **Structures**: Marine bases (wanted-poster walls), pirate ships, dojos.
- **Swords as rare loot** with the canon grades: Supreme (Yoru, black, cross-shaped, gold guard;
  Shodai Kitetsu), Great (Wado Ichimonji, white hilt and sheath; Shusui, black blade, black sheath;
  Enma, lilac with gold flowers; Ame no Habakiri, white), Grade (Sandai Kitetsu, cursed, red sheath,
  blue flame hamon); Kikoku (Law's ōdachi, fur tsuba), Gryphon (Shanks, green hilt, blue sheath),
  Murakumogiri (Whitebeard's naginata), Soul Solid (Brook's cane sword).
- **Items**: Den Den Mushi, Log Pose and Eternal Pose, Vivre Card (creeps towards its owner), Dials
  (Impact, Flame, Flash, Breath, Tone...), Kabuto, Clima-Tact, the Straw Hat, the Marine "Justice"
  coat, sake, Cola.
