        Punch a tree for logs and turn them into planks in your inventory's crafting grid. Four planks make a crafting table: right-click it for the full 3×3 grid, where a wooden pickaxe gets you mining. The green book lists every recipe and lays it out for you.

        Furnaces smelt ores and cook meat: the item goes on top, fuel below. Weapons need a moment between swings (the bar under the crosshair), so time your hits. You heal while your food bar is nearly full.

        To play with friends, open a world, press Esc and choose Open to Friends. On claude.ai, people you've shared this page with see your world under Multiplayer; anywhere else, give them the join code.

        On a touch screen the left stick walks, dragging looks around, a tap places and holding breaks.
