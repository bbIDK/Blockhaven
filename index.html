      How to play

      WASD
      Walk
      Mouse
      Look around
      Left click
      Break block (hold in Survival)
      Right click
      Place block, eat food, open doors and chests, sleep in a bed
      Space
      Jump — double-tap to fly in Creative
      Shift
      Sneak (won't fall off edges) / fly down
      Ctrl
      Sprint (or double-tap W)
      1–9, Wheel
      Choose hotbar slot
      E
      Inventory and crafting
      Middle click
      Pick the block you're looking at
      Q
      Drop the held item
      T, /
      Chat and commands (try /help)
      F3
      Coordinates and debug info
      Esc
      Pause

      Survival tip: punch a tree for logs, turn them into planks and a crafting table, then make a wooden pickaxe to mine stone. Place torches before night falls, or craft a bed from wool and planks to sleep through it. Sneak while right-clicking to place blocks against doors, chests and beds.

      On a touch screen: the left stick moves, drag anywhere else to look, tap to place, hold to break.
