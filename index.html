      Survival tip: punch a tree for logs, turn them into planks in your inventory's crafting grid, then make a crafting table. Right-click the table for the full 3×3 grid and craft a wooden pickaxe to mine stone. The green book button opens the recipe book, which lays recipes out for you. Sneak while right-clicking to place blocks against things you can use.

      Furnaces smelt ores, cook meat and bake sand into glass: put the item in the top slot and fuel (coal, charcoal, wood) in the bottom one. Weapons need a moment to wind up between hits; the meter under the crosshair shows when a swing will hit at full strength. Swords are quick, axes hit hard but slowly.

      Keep an eye on the drumsticks next to your hearts: you only heal when you're well fed. Cook meat from pigs, cows and chickens in a furnace for the most food. Hitting a mob while you're falling is a critical hit.

      On a touch screen: the left stick moves, drag anywhere else to look, tap to place, hold to break.
